About Solar_Clock

Solar_Clock redefines time. A “day” starts at sunrise and ends at the next sunrise. Daylight is mapped into AM hours, night into PM hours.

🌍 Strict Natural: AM and PM follow real intervals (varying lengths).
⚖️ Balanced 12/12: AM and PM always scaled to 12 hours each.

This clock lets the Sun set your rhythm — your local sky determines time, not conventional clocks.
