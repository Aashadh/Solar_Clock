About Sun-Clock

The Sun-Clock redefines time. A “day” starts at sunrise and ends at the next sunrise. Daylight is mapped into AM hours, night into PM hours.

🌍 Strict Natural: AM and PM vary with the seasons. ⚖️ Balanced: AM and PM are always 12h each, scaled to fit.

This clock follows the Sun — your local sky sets the rhythm, not midnight conventions.
