# 🌞 Solar-Clock

A natural clock that follows the **sun instead of time zones**.  
It redefines a "day" from **sunrise to sunrise**, splitting it into **AM (sunrise → sunset)** and **PM (sunset → next sunrise)**.  

Users can switch between two modes:
- **Strict Natural (🌍)**: AM and PM have real, unequal lengths (day vs night).  
- **Balanced (⚖️)**: AM and PM are scaled to exactly 12 hours each, while still syncing with sunrise/sunset.
